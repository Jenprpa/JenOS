# Human Capacity Model V1

## Principle

JenOS optimizes sustainable capacity, not maximum output.

Human Capacity is an established JenOS concern. Sprint 2.5 operationalizes it without redefining the Domain Model.

## Capacity Levels

V1 uses qualitative self-report:

- `low`
- `limited`
- `ok`
- `good`
- `unknown`

These are not scores.

They are gentle signals for decision support.

## Inputs

Capacity can be informed by owner-confirmed records such as:

- Sleep self-report.
- Meal status.
- Energy / readiness phrase.
- Existing commitments.
- Deadlines or calendar load.

## Prohibited Interpretations

JenOS must not:

- Diagnose the owner.
- Infer disease.
- Recommend medication.
- Score health.
- Score productivity.
- Shame missing data.
- Automatically cancel or stop a Project.

## Authority

The owner remains the decision maker.

JenOS may recommend, explain, and warn with evidence. It does not own the decision.
